## Production Notes for Songs 11-30

### Process Cluster (11-13)
- Style: Step-by-step, methodical
- Tempo: 85-90 BPM
- Clear sequential structure
- Emphasis on time connectors

### Map Cluster (14-16)
- Style: Spatial, geographical feel
- Tempo: 90-95 BPM
- Directional movement in melody
- Before/after contrast

### Proportions Cluster (17-18)
- Style: Mathematical, precise
- Tempo: 95 BPM
