# IELTS Paraphrasing Songs Collection

8 songs targeting challenges 31, 39, 60, 70: Paraphrasing recognition across Reading and Writing

**Purpose**: Help students recognize when the same idea is expressed in different words—critical for Reading comprehension and Writing vocabulary range.

---

## Song 1: "Up and Down the Mountain"
**Target**: Movement vocabulary (increase/decrease synonyms)

**Lexical Chunks:**
- **rose / increased / climbed / grew**
- **surged / soared / rocketed / escalated**
- **fell / decreased / declined / dropped**
- **plummeted / plunged / nosedived / crashed**
- **the numbers went up**
- **the figures went down**
- **experienced growth**
- **saw a reduction**

---

**[Verse 1 - Going Up]**
The temperature **rose** as summer approached that year,
it **increased** gradually, making the pattern clear,
sales **climbed** throughout the quarter without a pause,
profits **grew** steadily—everyone saw the cause

**[Chorus]**
Up and down the mountain, same path, different names,
**surged**, **soared**, **rocketed**—dramatic climbing games,
**fell**, **declined**, **dropped**, **plummeted** down fast,
paraphrase the movement, make your vocabulary last

**[Verse 2 - Going Down]**
Attendance **fell** after they raised the entrance fee,
it **decreased** by twenty percent, as we could see,
participation **declined** over the following months,
enrollment **dropped** sharply—all at once

**[Bridge - Extreme Movements]**
When it shoots up quickly: **surged**, **soared**, **rocketed** high,
**escalated** rapidly, reaching for the sky,
when it crashes down: **plummeted**, **plunged**, **nosedived** low,
steep and sudden—that's the flow

**[Verse 3 - Alternative Expressions]**
**The numbers went up** in every category measured,
**the figures went down** despite all efforts treasured,
the region **experienced growth** in tourism that season,
the company **saw a reduction** in costs for a reason

**[Final Chorus]**
One idea, many words—that's paraphrasing power,
**rose** = **increased** = **climbed** = **grew** every hour,
**fell** = **decreased** = **declined** = **dropped** the same,
IELTS hides answers by changing the name!

**[Outro - Reading Strategy]**
Question says "increased"? Text might say "rose"
Question says "fell"? Look for "declined" in prose
They mean the same—just different words,
paraphrasing tricks—learn to spot what you've heard!

## Usage Across IELTS Sections

### Reading (Challenges 31, 39)
- Recognize when question uses "increase" but passage says "rise"
- Match headings that paraphrase paragraph main ideas
- True/False/Not Given questions with paraphrased statements

### Writing Task 1 (Challenge 60)
- Avoid repetition: use synonyms for "increase/decrease"
- Demonstrate lexical range with variety of expressions
- Paraphrase the question in the overview

### Writing Task 2 (Challenge 70)
- Paraphrase the question in introduction
- Use varied vocabulary throughout essay
- Avoid repeating same words—show range

### Speaking
- Demonstrate vocabulary range by using synonyms
- Paraphrase examiner's questions in answers
- Use varied expressions instead of basic words
