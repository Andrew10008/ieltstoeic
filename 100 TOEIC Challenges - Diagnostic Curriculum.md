# 100 TOEIC Challenges - Diagnostic Curriculum

A comprehensive identification of specific problem areas students face when preparing for the TOEIC Listening and Reading Test.

---

## LISTENING PART 1 - PHOTOGRAPHS (10 challenges)

### Visual Interpretation

1. **Confusing active vs. passive voice descriptions** - "A man is pouring coffee" vs. "Coffee is being poured"
2. **Choosing answers with visible items not doing the action** - Hearing "phone" when phone is visible but not in use
3. **Missing progressive tense indicators** - Not catching "are being", "is being done"
4. **Confusing similar-sounding verbs** - "filing/filling", "working/walking"
5. **Focusing on people instead of actions** - Describing who, not what's happening

### Preposition & Location Issues

6. **Spatial preposition confusion** - "on/in/at", "next to/near/beside"
7. **Overlooking background vs. foreground** - Choosing answers about background elements
8. **Missing negative constructions** - "No one is sitting" when seats are empty
9. **Confusing singular/plural subjects** - "A person is" vs. "People are"
10. **Not recognizing passive with location** - "Items are arranged on the table"

---

## LISTENING PART 2 - QUESTION-RESPONSE (15 challenges)

### Question Type Recognition

11. **Mishearing question words** - Confusing "when/where/why/how"
12. **Falling for similar-sounding distractors** - Question about "time" answered with "location"
13. **Missing negative questions** - "Isn't it ready?" requires yes/no reversal
14. **Choosing responses with repeated words** - Picking answer that echoes question words
15. **Not recognizing indirect responses** - "Let me check" instead of direct yes/no

### Answer Pattern Understanding

16. **Expecting direct yes/no for all questions** - Missing alternative valid responses
17. **Confusion with tag questions** - "You're coming, aren't you?"
18. **Missing conditional responses** - "If... then..." answer structures
19. **Not catching suggestion alternatives** - "Why don't we" / "How about" responses
20. **Misunderstanding clarification requests** - "What do you mean?" as valid response

### Common Response Types

21. **Missing polite refusals** - "I'm afraid not" or "I'd rather not"
22. **Not recognizing time-buying responses** - "Let me think about it"
23. **Confusion with either/or questions** - Expecting one choice, getting "both" or "neither"
24. **Missing opinion expressions** - "I think", "In my view", "Personally"
25. **Not catching uncertainty markers** - "I'm not sure", "Possibly", "Maybe"

---

## LISTENING PART 3 - CONVERSATIONS (15 challenges)

### Multi-speaker Tracking

26. **Losing track of who said what** - Confusing speakers' roles
27. **Missing speaker agreement/disagreement** - Not catching opinions
28. **Not identifying speaker intentions** - Missing requests, suggestions, complaints
29. **Confusion about topic changes** - Not following conversation flow
30. **Missing implied information** - Inferring what's not directly stated

### Business Context Understanding

31. **Unfamiliar with office scenarios** - Not understanding workplace situations
32. **Missing meeting-related vocabulary** - "Postpone", "reschedule", "agenda"
33. **Confusion with business roles** - "Manager", "supervisor", "coordinator"
34. **Not catching problem-solution patterns** - Missing what issue was and how it's solved
35. **Unfamiliar with phone conversation etiquette** - "May I take a message?"

### Question Types

36. **Visual question interpretation** - Questions referring to charts/schedules
37. **Missing inference questions** - "What will the man probably do next?"
38. **Not catching attitude/opinion questions** - "What does the woman think about...?"
39. **Confusion with detail questions** - Specific facts vs. main ideas
40. **Missing NOT/EXCEPT questions** - Choosing what IS mentioned instead

---

## LISTENING PART 4 - TALKS (10 challenges)

### Talk Type Recognition

41. **Not identifying announcement type** - Missing context clues
42. **Confusion with talk purpose** - Advertise vs. inform vs. instruct
43. **Missing target audience indicators** - Who the talk is for
44. **Not recognizing talk settings** - Airport, museum, office, store
45. **Confusion with multiple-topic talks** - Talks covering several points

### Information Processing

46. **Cannot hold multiple details in memory** - Forgetting earlier information
47. **Missing signpost language** - "First", "Next", "Finally", "However"
48. **Not catching time/schedule information** - Opening hours, dates, deadlines
49. **Confusion with number details** - Prices, percentages, quantities
50. **Missing implied next steps** - "What are listeners asked to do?"

---

## READING PART 5 - INCOMPLETE SENTENCES (20 challenges)

### Grammar Issues

51. **Subject-verb agreement errors** - Plural subject with singular verb
52. **Verb tense confusion** - Present vs. past vs. present perfect
53. **Preposition choice mistakes** - "Interested in/on/to"
54. **Article usage errors** - a/an/the or no article
55. **Confusing adjective vs. adverb** - "-ly" form decisions

### Vocabulary & Word Forms

56. **Wrong word form selection** - Noun vs. verb vs. adjective form
57. **Confusing similar words** - "affect/effect", "complement/compliment"
58. **Missing collocations** - "Make a decision" not "do a decision"
59. **Transition word errors** - "However" vs. "Therefore" vs. "Moreover"
60. **Not recognizing word family** - "Manage, manager, management, managerial"

### Sentence Structure

61. **Relative pronoun confusion** - "Who" vs. "which" vs. "that"
62. **Infinitive vs. gerund errors** - "Enjoy doing" vs. "want to do"
63. **Passive voice recognition** - When to use "be + past participle"
64. **Conditional structure mistakes** - If-clause verb forms
65. **Comparative/superlative errors** - "More better" or wrong form

### Context Understanding

66. **Ignoring sentence context** - Not reading full sentence
67. **Missing time markers** - "Yesterday", "next week" indicate tense
68. **Not checking answer fit** - Grammatically correct but illogical
69. **Overlooking subject plurality** - Not matching verb to actual subject
70. **Missing logical connectors** - Relationship between clauses

---

## READING PART 6 - TEXT COMPLETION (10 challenges)

### Sentence-Level Issues

71. **Not reading surrounding sentences** - Missing context for gap
72. **Choosing grammatically correct but contextually wrong** - Fits grammar, not meaning
73. **Missing pronoun reference** - What "it" or "they" refers to
74. **Ignoring paragraph topic** - Answer doesn't match paragraph focus
75. **Not checking tense consistency** - Answer tense doesn't match text

### Whole-Sentence Insertion

76. **Not identifying where sentence belongs** - Wrong position in text
77. **Missing logical flow indicators** - "However", "Therefore" mismatched
78. **Not checking subject continuity** - Sentence subject doesn't connect
79. **Ignoring transition logic** - Sentence doesn't bridge ideas
80. **Missing time sequence** - Sentence disrupts chronological order

---

## READING PART 7 - READING COMPREHENSION (20 challenges)

### Single Passage Questions

81. **Not scanning for specific information** - Reading entire text for details
82. **Missing synonym paraphrasing** - Question says "purchase", text says "buy"
83. **Choosing answers with text keywords** - Word matching trap
84. **Not identifying text purpose** - Advertisement vs. notice vs. email
85. **Missing NOT/TRUE questions** - Selecting what IS true when asked for NOT

### Double/Triple Passage Questions

86. **Not identifying which text contains answer** - Searching wrong passage
87. **Missing connections between texts** - How texts relate
88. **Confusion with cross-reference information** - Information split across texts
89. **Not checking all passages for answers** - Stopping after first text
90. **Missing contradictions between texts** - Different information in passages

### Inference & Detail Questions

91. **Cannot infer implied information** - Only seeing literal meaning
92. **Missing "probably/likely" questions** - Not making logical deductions
93. **Confusion with attitude/purpose questions** - Why was text written?
94. **Not catching specific detail questions** - When, where, who, what questions
95. **Missing vocabulary-in-context questions** - Meaning based on usage

### Time Management

96. **Spending too long on difficult passages** - Not moving on when stuck
97. **Not prioritizing easier questions** - Tackling hardest first
98. **Failing to skim before detailed reading** - No overview first
99. **Not tracking time across 54 questions** - Running out of time
100. **Not guessing on unknown answers** - Leaving blanks (no penalty for guessing)

## ADDITIONAL COMMON ERRORS (Not in main 100, but critical)

### Grammar Foundations
- Modal verb usage (can/could/should/must)
- Quantifier confusion (much/many, few/little)
- Reported speech transformations
- Word order in questions
- Parallel structure in lists

### Vocabulary Gaps
- Business email phrases
- Office equipment terminology
- Meeting and conference vocabulary
- Financial and budget terms
- Customer service expressions

### Test Strategy
- Not reading all four answer choices
- Second-guessing correct answers
- Spending unequal time across sections
- Not using process of elimination
- Failing to practice with actual test format

## Usage Guide

This curriculum can be used to:

1. **Diagnostic Assessment** - Identify specific student weaknesses
2. **Targeted Practice** - Address challenges systematically
3. **Progress Tracking** - Monitor improvement across specific areas
4. **Curriculum Design** - Build courses around these 100 points
5. **Material Development** - Create songs, exercises, and lessons per challenge

Each challenge represents:
- A specific teachable moment
- Potential for a focused lesson (10-15 minutes)
- Basis for practice exercises
- Foundation for educational songs
- Assessment question opportunities
