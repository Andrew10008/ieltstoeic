# 100 IELTS Challenges - Diagnostic Curriculum

A comprehensive identification of specific problem areas students face when preparing for IELTS Academic.

---

## LISTENING CHALLENGES (20)

### Section Strategy & Question Types

1. **Not previewing questions during preparation time** - Missing the chance to predict answers
2. **Failing to identify question types quickly** - Wasting time understanding what's being asked
3. **Missing answers that appear in order** - Not realizing IELTS listening is sequential
4. **Confusion between speakers in multi-person dialogues** - Losing track of who said what
5. **Falling for distractor information** - Writing the first answer heard, missing corrections

### Word Recognition & Spelling

6. **Mishearing similar-sounding words** - Confusing "fifteen/fifty", "can/can't"
7. **Spelling errors on correctly heard answers** - Writing "Febuary" instead of "February"
8. **Plural/singular confusion** - Writing "book" when "books" was said
9. **Not catching contracted forms** - Missing "won't", "could've", "I'd"
10. **Unable to recognize paraphrasing** - Question says "vehicle" but audio says "car"

### Accent & Speed Issues

11. **Struggling with British accents** - When trained only on American English
12. **Difficulty understanding Australian pronunciation** - Unfamiliar vowel sounds
13. **Cannot follow fast native speech** - Natural speed overwhelming
14. **Missing linked words in connected speech** - "going to" → "gonna"
15. **Trouble with reduced weak forms** - "and" → "n", "of" → "uh"

### Focus & Completion

16. **Losing concentration mid-section** - Mind wandering causes missed answers
17. **Panicking after missing one answer** - Anxiety cascades into more mistakes
18. **Not transferring answers accurately** - Making errors copying to answer sheet
19. **Forgetting to check word limits** - Writing "three words" when limit is "two words"
20. **Leaving answers blank instead of guessing** - Not attempting all questions

## READING CHALLENGES (20)

### Time Management

21. **Not finishing all 40 questions in 60 minutes** - Spending too long on early passages
22. **Reading every word instead of skimming** - Wasting time on detailed reading
23. **Spending too long on difficult questions** - Not moving on when stuck
24. **No time allocation strategy** - Not dividing time across three passages
25. **Rushing the final passage** - Running out of time for Passage 3

### Question Type Difficulties

26. **Confusing True/False/Not Given** - Choosing False when answer is Not Given
27. **Struggling with Matching Headings** - Unable to identify paragraph main ideas
28. **Multiple Choice distractor confusion** - Choosing answers with keywords from text
29. **Sentence completion grammatical errors** - Answers that don't fit grammatically
30. **Summary completion vocabulary gaps** - Not recognizing paraphrased words

### Reading Skills

31. **Cannot identify paraphrasing** - Missing that "increase" = "rise" = "grow"
32. **Poor scanning for specific information** - Taking too long to locate names/dates
33. **Weak skimming for main ideas** - Unable to grasp passage overview quickly
34. **Getting lost in complex sentence structures** - Long academic sentences confuse
35. **Limited academic vocabulary** - Too many unknown words per passage

### Strategy & Understanding

36. **Not reading questions before the passage** - Missing the purpose of reading
37. **Trying to understand everything** - Not selective about what to comprehend
38. **Ignoring question keywords** - Not underlining important terms
39. **Falling for word-matching traps** - Choosing answers with exact text words
40. **Not checking answers fit the question** - Grammatically incorrect completions

## WRITING TASK 1 CHALLENGES (15)

### Task Understanding

41. **Not writing an overview statement** - Missing the most important feature
42. **Including personal opinion in Task 1** - Adding subjective views (forbidden)
43. **Describing every single detail** - Not selecting key features
44. **Ignoring obvious trends** - Missing the main pattern in data
45. **Misinterpreting the data/diagram** - Reading charts incorrectly

### Language & Vocabulary

46. **Overusing simple sentence structures** - Only "The number was X" repeatedly
47. **Repetitive vocabulary** - Using "increase" 10 times instead of synonyms
48. **Incorrect prepositions with trends** - "Increased in" instead of "increased by"
49. **Verb tense errors in descriptions** - Using wrong tense for time periods
50. **Lack of comparison language** - Not using "whereas", "while", "compared to"

### Organization & Coherence

51. **No clear paragraph structure** - Writing one long block of text
52. **Poor use of cohesive devices** - No linking words or too many
53. **Not grouping similar information** - Describing items randomly
54. **Weak introduction** - Not paraphrasing the question
55. **Under or over word count** - Writing less than 150 or too much (200+)

---

## WRITING TASK 2 CHALLENGES (20)

### Task Response

56. **Not addressing all parts of the question** - Discussing only causes, ignoring solutions
57. **Unclear position/opinion** - Examiner can't tell what you think
58. **Going off-topic** - Writing about tangential issues
59. **Not fully developing main ideas** - Stating points without explanation
60. **Using irrelevant examples** - Examples that don't support the argument

### Sentence Structure & Grammar

61. **Overusing simple sentences** - No complex structures (subordinate clauses)
62. **Run-on sentences** - Joining too many clauses incorrectly
63. **Sentence fragments** - Incomplete sentences
64. **Subject-verb agreement errors** - "People is", "The government are"
65. **Article errors** - Missing "a/the" or using them incorrectly

### Vocabulary Issues

66. **Repetitive word choice** - Using "good" and "bad" throughout
67. **Inappropriate informal language** - "kids", "lots of", "a bunch of"
68. **Misusing advanced vocabulary** - Using words incorrectly to impress
69. **Lack of topic-specific vocabulary** - Generic words for specialized topics
70. **Poor paraphrasing of the question** - Copying the prompt exactly

### Organization & Development

71. **No clear paragraph structure** - Ideas jumbled across paragraphs
72. **Weak topic sentences** - Paragraphs lack clear focus
73. **Insufficient explanation of ideas** - One-sentence paragraphs
74. **Missing introduction or conclusion** - Incomplete essay structure
75. **Overusing memorized phrases** - Template language obvious to examiners

### Grammar & Accuracy

76. **Tense inconsistency** - Switching between past and present randomly
77. **Pronoun reference errors** - Unclear what "it" or "they" refers to
78. **Preposition mistakes** - "Depend of", "interested to"
79. **Relative clause errors** - Incorrect use of who/which/that
80. **Conditional structure mistakes** - Wrong verb forms in if-clauses

---

## SPEAKING CHALLENGES (25)

### Part 1 - Introduction & Interview

81. **One-word or very short answers** - "Yes" without elaboration
82. **Not extending answers with reasons** - Missing opportunities to speak more
83. **Memorized-sounding responses** - Robotic delivery of prepared answers
84. **Unnatural formality** - Speaking like an essay instead of conversation
85. **Hesitation and long pauses** - Excessive "um", "uh", silence

### Part 2 - Long Turn (Cue Card)

86. **Running out of content before 2 minutes** - Only speaking for 30-40 seconds
87. **Not using the preparation time effectively** - Poor or no notes
88. **Ignoring parts of the cue card** - Not addressing all bullet points
89. **Going completely off-topic** - Talking about something unrelated
90. **Repeating the same point** - Saying the same thing multiple ways (no content)

### Part 3 - Two-way Discussion

91. **Not understanding abstract questions** - Confusion with hypothetical scenarios
92. **Giving Part 1-level short answers** - Not developing ideas for Part 3
93. **Unable to express opinions clearly** - Vague or contradictory positions
94. **Lack of examples to support points** - Abstract claims without illustration
95. **Not comparing or contrasting** - Missing "in the past vs. now" questions

### Fluency & Coherence

96. **Breaking sentences mid-thought** - Starting over repeatedly
97. **Lack of discourse markers** - No "well", "actually", "for instance"
98. **Jumping between ideas randomly** - No logical flow
99. **Over-reliance on simple connectors** - Only using "and", "but", "so"
100. **Self-correcting too frequently** - Constant grammar self-correction disrupts flow

### Pronunciation

101. **Monotone delivery** - No intonation variation
102. **Word stress on wrong syllables** - "PHOtograph" instead of "phoTOgraph"
103. **Missing final consonants** - "las night" instead of "last night"
104. **Confusing minimal pairs** - "rice/lice", "ship/sheep"
105. **Unclear articulation** - Mumbling or speaking too quietly

### Lexical Resource

106. **Repetitive basic vocabulary** - "good", "nice", "very" overused
107. **Searching for words mid-sentence** - Long pauses for vocabulary
108. **Using wrong words** - "I'm boring" instead of "I'm bored"
109. **No idiomatic expressions** - Only literal language (Band 6 limit)
110. **Inability to paraphrase** - Can't explain when a word is forgotten

### Grammar

111. **Tense errors when telling stories** - Mixing past and present
112. **Missing third person -s** - "He work" instead of "He works"
113. **Incorrect question formation** - "Where you went?" instead of "Where did you go?"
114. **Articles omission or misuse** - "I am teacher" / "The life is good"
115. **Plural errors** - "informations", "advices", "furnitures"

## GENERAL TEST STRATEGY CHALLENGES (10)

116. **Not understanding band descriptors** - Don't know what examiners look for
117. **No practice under timed conditions** - Always practicing with unlimited time
118. **Not reviewing mistakes** - Repeating the same errors without analysis
119. **Over-relying on templates** - Using memorized structures rigidly
120. **Poor exam day time management** - Not knowing how to pace each section
121. **Test anxiety affecting performance** - Nerves preventing best performance
122. **Not familiar with answer sheet** - Making transfer errors
123. **Skipping instructions** - Not reading task requirements carefully
124. **No awareness of question weighting** - Spending equal time on all tasks
125. **Lack of self-assessment** - Unable to identify own weak areas

## Usage Guide

This curriculum can be used to:

1. **Diagnostic Assessment** - Identify which specific challenges a student faces
2. **Targeted Teaching** - Address problems one by one systematically
3. **Progress Tracking** - Check off challenges as they're overcome
4. **Song/Material Creation** - Create educational content targeting each challenge
5. **Personalized Study Plans** - Focus on student's specific weak areas

Each challenge represents an opportunity for focused improvement and could be the basis for:
- A mini-lesson (10-15 minutes)
- Practice exercises
- An educational song
- Assessment questions
- Tutorial videos
