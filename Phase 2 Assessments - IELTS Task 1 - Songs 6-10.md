## Assessment 11
**Assessment number:** 11
**Sub-assessment type:** IELTS 1-50
**Discourse marker:** Basic Downward Trends - "fell/decreased/declined/dropped"

**Brief meaning:**
• **fell**
• **decreased**
• **declined**
• **dropped**
• **experienced a downward trend**

**Pre-listening:** "Just as you vary vocabulary for increases, you must vary vocabulary for decreases. Band 7+ writers use fell, decreased, declined, and dropped interchangeably to avoid repetition."

**Usage Guide:**
**MEANING FOCUS:**
Band 5-6 writers repeat "decreased" or use informal "went down." Band 7+ writers vary naturally: fell, declined, dropped.

**Band 5-6:** "Sales decreased in Q1. Profits decreased in Q2. Revenue decreased in Q3."
**Band 7+:** "Sales fell in Q1, while profits declined in Q2. Subsequently, revenue dropped in Q3."

**Practice Scenarios:**

**Situation 1:** Describing unemployment reduction
Band 5-6: "Unemployment decreased from 10% to 5%."
Band 7+: "Unemployment fell from 10% to 5% over the five-year period."

**Situation 2:** Multiple downward trends
Band 5-6: "Manufacturing jobs decreased. Coal production decreased too."
Band 7+: "Manufacturing jobs declined steadily, while coal production likewise dropped throughout the decade."

**Situation 3:** Using the phrase form
Band 5-6: "The economy got worse."
Band 7+: "The economy experienced a downward trend, with GDP declining consistently."

**Recording Tips:**

**Avoid:**
• Using only "decreased" or "went down" ✗
• Informal phrases like "got lower" ✗
• Confusing decrease/decline (they're interchangeable) ✗

**Use:**
• Vary between fell/decreased/declined/dropped ✓
• Use past simple for completed actions ✓
• Add adverbs for precision (fell sharply, declined gradually) ✓

**Recording Task:**
Transform this repetitive description: "Emissions decreased in 2010. They decreased again in 2015. They decreased more in 2020." → "Emissions fell in 2010, declined further in 2015, and dropped substantially by 2020."

## Assessment 17
**Assessment number:** 17
**Sub-assessment type:** IELTS 1-50
**Discourse marker:** Leveling Off - "plateaued at/leveled off"

**Brief meaning:**
• **plateaued at**
• **leveled off at**
• **stabilized at**
• **reached a plateau of**

**Pre-listening:** "A plateau describes when something rises or falls, then becomes flat. This is different from being constant throughout - it shows a change followed by stability."

**Usage Guide:**
**MEANING FOCUS:**
Band 5-6 writers miss the two-phase pattern: "increased then stopped." Band 7+ writers use "plateaued" to show the transition from change to stability.

**Band 5-6:** "Sales increased until 2015. Then they stopped increasing."
**Band 7+:** "Sales increased until 2015, when they plateaued at approximately 500,000 units."

**Practice Scenarios:**

**Situation 1:** Describing growth then stability
Band 5-6: "Population grew until 2010 then stayed the same."
Band 7+: "Population grew steadily until 2010, when it leveled off at around 8 million."

**Situation 2:** Multiple plateaus
Band 5-6: "The graph went up, then flat, then up again, then flat."
Band 7+: "The data showed two distinct plateaus: first stabilizing at 1,000 in 2005, then rising again before plateauing at 1,500 in 2015."

**Situation 3:** Plateau after decrease
Band 5-6: "Unemployment fell then stopped falling."
Band 7+: "Unemployment declined until 2012, then plateaued at approximately 4% for the remainder of the period."

**Recording Tips:**

**Avoid:**
• Saying "stopped increasing/decreasing" ✗
• Missing the two-phase nature (change → stability) ✗
• Using "plateau" for data that was always stable ✗

**Use:**
• "Plateaued at" + specific value ✓
• Use for data that changed THEN became stable ✓
• "Leveled off" is interchangeable with "plateaued" ✓

**Recording Task:**
Improve this description: "House prices went up from 2000 to 2007, then they stopped going up." → "House prices rose steadily from 2000 to 2007, when they plateaued at approximately $250,000."

**Usage Guide:**
**MEANING FOCUS:**
Band 5-6 writers always put adverbs in the same position: "significantly increased, significantly decreased." Band 7+ writers vary: "increased significantly," "showed significant growth."

**Band 5-6:** "Sales significantly increased. Profits significantly increased. Revenue significantly increased."
**Band 7+:** "Sales increased significantly, while profits rose sharply. Revenue demonstrated substantial growth."

**Practice Scenarios:**

**Situation 1:** Varying adverb position
Band 5-6: "Rapidly increased... rapidly increased... rapidly increased..."
Band 7+: "Increased rapidly... rose quickly... demonstrated rapid growth... experienced a swift increase."

**Situation 2:** Using noun phrases
Band 5-6: "Population steadily grew."
Band 7+: "Population demonstrated steady growth" OR "showed a steady increase."

**Situation 3:** Maximum variation
Band 5-6: "Dramatically fell, dramatically decreased, dramatically dropped."
Band 7+: "Fell dramatically, plummeted, experienced a sharp decline, showed a dramatic decrease."

**Recording Tips:**

**Avoid:**
• Always putting adverbs in the same position ✗
• Using only verb forms (missing noun phrase variations) ✗
• Repetitive patterns that examiner notices ✗

**Use:**
• Verb + adverb: "increased significantly" ✓
• Adverb + verb: "significantly increased" ✓
• Noun phrase: "showed significant growth" ✓
• Vary throughout your response ✓

**Recording Task:**
Transform this repetitive text: "Exports significantly increased in 2010. Imports significantly increased in 2012. Trade volume significantly increased in 2015." → "Exports increased significantly in 2010. Two years later, imports rose sharply. By 2015, overall trade volume had demonstrated substantial growth."
